# Kobo Clara HD MeshCore Grip

A handheld grip that turns a Kobo Clara HD into a MeshCore device without touching it. 71 × 117 × 22.4 mm, one PETG piece, no screws.

- **The Kobo stays whole.** Hold it in landscape and slide its chin (the wide bezel at its bottom edge) sideways into the grip's slot. It just sits there, and comes out the same way.
- **The slot.** A 2 mm lip covers the chin in front, stopping 2 mm short of the screen. Behind, the grip's floor reaches about 42 mm in. The grip's top and bottom walls close its ends.
- **Cylinder.** An 18650 (with wire leads) lies in a cylinder up the right side. The cylinder is flush with the front and stands out at the back. A half-ring above the bay stops the cell sliding up, and the leads pass inside it.
- **Open back.** The grip's depth carries on inward behind the Kobo to a rounded inner end. The back is open, so everything inside is reached from behind.
- **XIAOs.** Three Seeed XIAO boards (an nRF and two SX1262 radios) lie flat on the floor behind the chin. They're stacked down from the top wall, against the cylinder's side.
- **Two SMA bulkheads** stand in the top wall, the jack's hex under the wall and the nut on top:
  - over the cylinder: the jack goes up the cylinder from below, its hex in a hex hole;
  - in the top of the rounded inner end: the jack slides in from behind, into a hex channel and a slot through the top wall.
- **Strip antenna.** One radio can use an internal strip antenna instead. It sticks inside the rounded inner end, below the second SMA's mount (about 85 mm of wall).
- **Power button.** It sits at the bottom of a 45° trough cut down through the front.

The Kobo's outline and thickness (159.6 × 110 × 8.35 mm) are Kobo's spec. The screen's position (so where the lip stops) and the power button's position are **estimates from Kobo's drawing**. Measure them before printing.

## Printing

Print standing on its bottom end, the front face to one side. Every wall runs straight up, so it needs no supports:
- The slot's top end bridges 8.65 mm between the lip and the floor.
- Both SMA mounts and the power button trough's top end rise at 45°.
- The cell stop's underside is a 45° cone.
- The bottom corners are octagonal.
