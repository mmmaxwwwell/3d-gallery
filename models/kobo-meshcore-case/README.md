# Kobo Clara HD MeshCore Case

A prototype case that turns a Kobo Clara HD into a MeshCore handheld: the Kobo is the screen, and an nRF board, LoRa radio and battery ride in a grip on its right.

- **Landscape, grip on the right.** The Kobo sits in a frame that lips 2 mm over its bezel. To its right a bay 38 mm wide and 24 mm thick (the Kobo section is 12.65 mm) holds the electronics, and doubles as the handle.
- **Antenna.** An SMA female bulkhead pigtail goes through a 6.6 mm hole in the bay's top wall, so the antenna stands straight up at the top right.
- **Kobo's bottom edge faces the bay.** Its micro USB opens into the bay through a 13 mm window in the divider, so a short OTG cable can run to the MCU. Its power button sits at the bottom of a 45° trough cut down through the front, where you press it sideways with a fingertip.
- **Three PETG pieces, eight M3 cap screws.** The frame prints face down. The back plate (4 × M3x8) and the bay lid (4 × M3x10) print flat, with counterbores in pads on their outsides. The screws self-tap 2.5 mm pilots. Overall 216 × 117 mm.

The Kobo's outline and thickness (159.6 × 110 × 8.35 mm) are Kobo's spec. The screen window, the corner radius, and where the power button and USB sit along the edge are **estimates from Kobo's drawing**. Measure them before printing.

## Printing

| Piece | Orientation | Plate |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | front face down | 1 |
| Back plate | inner face down, pads up | 2 |
| Bay lid | inner face down, pads up | 2 |

No supports. Every overhang is 45° or less: the USB window and the SMA hole are peaked at 45°, and the button trough slopes at 45°.
