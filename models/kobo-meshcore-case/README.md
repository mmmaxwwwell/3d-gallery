# Kobo Clara HD MeshCore Case

A slim prototype case that turns a Kobo Clara HD into a MeshCore handheld. 195 × 117 mm overall. It's 12.65 mm thick behind the Kobo and 22.4 mm at the cylinder.

- **Frame.** The Kobo sits in landscape in a frame that lips 2 mm over its bezel.
- **18650 cylinder.** It runs up the Kobo's right side, flush with the front face, and stands out at the back. It splits at its axis: the front half is part of the frame, the back half part of the electronics cover. With the cover off, the cell (with its wire leads) lifts out of the back. A half-ring over the bay stops it sliding up, and the leads pass inside the ring.
- **Antenna.** The cylinder stops 25 mm below the top of the case in a hex mount. The SMA bulkhead pigtail's 8 mm hex drops into the front half of a pocket under the end panel, and the cover closes over the back half. The nut tightens on top of the panel, which clamps the two halves together, and the antenna stands in the open on the cylinder's axis. The cylinder's top end is rounded, and so are the cover's edge and corner beside the antenna.
- **Electronics cover.** The cylinder's depth carries on 48 mm inward behind the Kobo, the case's whole height. One flat back runs across both, and the inner end rounds down to the back plate.
- **XIAOs.** Two Seeed XIAO boards (no headers) lie flat on the back plate near the top, one above the other, USB-C ends to the left. Four L-shaped corner posts locate each board and leave its pad edges free for wires. Posts inside the cover hold the boards down: on the USB-C connector at one end and on the PCB at the other.
- **Wiring.** A window through the cover's right rib, between the cell and the SMA, carries the pigtail's cable and the cell's leads to the boards.
- **Power button.** It sits at the bottom of a 45° trough cut down through the front. The Kobo's micro USB isn't used.
- **Screws.** Seven M3:
  - Back plate's left end: 2 × M3x8 into the frame's left wall, counterbored in pads.
  - Beside the cylinder: 2 × M3x16 down through the cover's right rib and the plate into the frame.
  - Cover's inner end: 2 × M3x6 into bosses on the plate.
  - Cylinder's bottom: 1 × M3x10 through both halves.

The Kobo's outline and thickness (159.6 × 110 × 8.35 mm) are Kobo's spec. The screen window, the corner radius and the power button's position are **estimates from Kobo's drawing**. Measure them before printing.

## Printing

| Piece | Orientation | Plate |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | front face down | 1 |
| Back plate | inner face down, posts and bosses up | 2 |
| Electronics cover | standing on its bottom end | 2 |

No supports:
- The frame's half of the cylinder is an open trough.
- Its front-right quarter is a 45° flat, so it doesn't overhang the bed.
- The cover stands on its end, so its round end, the cylinder's back half and the ribs are plain walls.
- The cover's screw holes are teardrops, apart from the bottom block's, which is a small round hole.
- The bay narrows to the hex pocket at 45°.

The rest are short bridges: the window's top, the hex pocket's roof and the top end wall.
