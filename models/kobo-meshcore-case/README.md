# Kobo Clara HD MeshCore Case

A slim prototype case that turns a Kobo Clara HD into a MeshCore handheld. 195 × 117 mm overall. It's 12.65 mm thick behind the Kobo and 22.4 mm at the thick part.

- **Frame.** The Kobo sits in landscape in a frame that lips 2 mm over its bezel.
- **Thick part.** It runs the case's whole height on the right. An 18650 cylinder is flush with the front face and stands out at the back, and its depth carries on 48 mm inward behind the Kobo.
  - It's an open tub: the frame is its outer wall and the back is open, so everything inside is reached from behind.
  - The cell (with its wire leads) lies in a trough. A half-ring above the bay stops it sliding up, and the leads pass inside the ring.
  - The right side's top edge is rounded, and so are its corners in plan.
- **SMA.** It mounts in the top of the cylinder, at the top of the case, all in the frame. The pigtail's 8 mm hex sits in a pocket under the top wall, and the nut tightens on top. There's about 38 mm of room below it for the jack and its cable.
- **Back plate.** One piece. It covers the Kobo and stops where the thick part starts. It carries the tub's inner end, a rounded wall rising to the rim. Two strips reach across the Kobo's back to screw down beside the cylinder.
- **XIAOs.** The top strip has a window for each of two Seeed XIAO boards (no headers). They lie flat against the Kobo's back near the antenna, one above the other, 0.2 mm clear all round.
- **Power button.** It sits at the bottom of a 45° trough cut down through the front. The Kobo's micro USB isn't used.
- **Screws.** Four M3x8, counterbored in pads on the back plate: two into the frame's left wall, two into the ledge beside the cylinder.

The Kobo's outline and thickness (159.6 × 110 × 8.35 mm) are Kobo's spec. The screen window, the corner radius and the power button's position are **estimates from Kobo's drawing**. Measure them before printing.

## Printing

| Piece | Orientation | Plate |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | front face down | 1 |
| Back plate | inner face down, the rounded wall and pads up | 2 |

No supports:
- The tub opens upward as the frame prints.
- The cylinder's front-right quarter is a 45° flat.
- The hex pocket's roof is a short flat bridge.
- The back plate's rounded wall is solid, straight up on its inside.
- All holes are round.
