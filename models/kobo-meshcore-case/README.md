# Kobo Clara HD MeshCore Case

A slim prototype case that turns a Kobo Clara HD into a MeshCore handheld.

- **Landscape, a tube up the right side.** The Kobo sits in a frame that lips 2 mm over its bezel. Up its right side runs a 15 mm tube, flush with the front face. It's thicker than the frame, so it stands out at the back: the case is 10.65 mm thick behind the Kobo, 12.65 mm with the back plate, and 15 mm at the tube. Overall 184 × 117 mm.
- **Antenna.** An SMA female bulkhead pigtail mounts on a 2 mm panel 25 mm down an 11 mm bore in the tube's top, so the antenna's base sits recessed on the tube's axis. Push the pigtail's jack up the 10 mm bore from the tube's bottom end, thread through the panel, and tighten the nut from the top. In the recess that takes a deep socket or long-nose pliers. The cable leaves through the bottom of the tube.
- **Kobo's bottom edge faces the tube.** Its micro USB comes out through a 13 mm window across the tube, so a cable plugs in from the right. Its power button sits at the bottom of a 45° trough cut down through the front, where you press it sideways with a fingertip.
- **Two PETG pieces, four M3x8 cap screws.** The back plate screws into the frame's left wall and the strip beside the tube, with counterbores in pads on its outside.

The Kobo's outline and thickness (159.6 × 110 × 8.35 mm) are Kobo's spec. The screen window, the corner radius, and where the power button and USB sit along the edge are **estimates from Kobo's drawing**. Measure them before printing.

## Printing

| Piece | Orientation | Plate |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | front face down; the tube lies along the bed | 1 |
| Back plate | inner face down, pads up | 2 |

No supports:
- The tube's front-right quarter is a 45° flat, so it doesn't overhang the bed.
- The bores and the USB window are 45° teardrops, flat-topped 1.5 mm under the tube's back: short bridges.
- The button trough slopes at 45°.
