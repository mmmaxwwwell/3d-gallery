# MOLLE 18650 Holder

Four 18650 cells standing upright, each in a thin tube of its own, the tubes
fused side by side onto a MOLLE stick weave. The whole thing goes straight
onto PALS webbing with two of BigFootPL's
[Molle sticks](https://www.printables.com/model/411201-molle-stick) (in the
gallery's Library, `molle-stick`), the way a MOLLE pouch does. There is
nothing to screw together.

## Mounting

- Print two **3"** sticks. The holder covers one PALS band, two columns
  across (86 × 79.5 × 31.8 mm, plus the sticks' heads).
- Hold the holder against the webbing with its band window over a band: one
  block sits in the PALS gap above it, one in the gap below.
- Push each stick down through the slot in the top block, behind the band
  and through the bottom block. The slots are open at the bottom, so a
  longer stick works too; it just pokes out below. The hook
  springs over the top block's back wall, and the bulge on its outer leaf
  clicks into the groove across the wall's back.
- To take it off, pull the stick up by its head.

## Cells

Drop each cell in from the top: the rounded mouth guides it, and it snaps
past the bump on the lever in the back of its tube. The lever is a 40 mm
strip of the tube's back wall, freed by two slits, and it swings back into
a pocket inside the holder. A ring of whole wall over it means a cell thrown
in hits the ring, not the bump, and the pocket stops the lever before it
bends too far. To take a cell out, push it up with a finger through the slot
down the tube's front, or from underneath, where the slot runs on through
the floor. Two small crush ribs stop it rattling, and water runs out of the
slot.

## Printing

PETG, standing on its bottom, no supports: 4 walls, 15% gyroid. The tubes,
slots, levers and their pockets run straight up, the top block's underside
over the band is 45°, and so are the bumps' undersides. Every outside edge
is rounded; the bed edge is chamfered in layer steps.

## Customizing

Cell count, cell diameter and length, the ribs' squeeze, the wall, the
finger slot's width, the levers' length and width and how far their bumps
reach, and the
stick length: 3" on one band, 4" or 5" on two, 6" or 7" on three. Protected
or button-top cells up to about 69 mm fit on the 3".

Not yet printed: the clearances, the crush ribs' squeeze, the levers'
bumps and the leaf's click are untested.
