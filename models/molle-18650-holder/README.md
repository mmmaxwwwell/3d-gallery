# MOLLE 18650 Holder

Four 18650 cells stand upright side by side in one printed piece that goes
straight onto PALS webbing with two of BigFootPL's
[Molle sticks](https://www.printables.com/model/411201-molle-stick) (in the
gallery's Library, `molle-stick`), the way a MOLLE pouch does. There is
nothing to screw together and no bolt grid, so the back matches the PALS
spacing exactly.

## Mounting

- Print two **4"** sticks. The holder covers two PALS bands, two columns
  across (86 × 104.5 mm).
- Hold the holder against the webbing with a PALS gap behind each of its
  three blocks: one block above the top band, one between the bands, one
  below the bottom band.
- Push each stick down through the slot in the top block, behind the top band,
  through the middle block, behind the next band and into the bottom block,
  until its tip sits on the floor there. The hook snaps over the top block's
  back wall.
- To take it off, lift the hook off the wall and pull the stick up.

## Cells

Drop the cells in from the top. Two small crush ribs in each pocket hold the
cell; the bevel across the top front leaves each cell's top showing, so you
can grip it and pull it out. A drain hole under each cell lets water out,
and a pen pushed up through it frees a stuck cell.

## Printing

PETG, front face down, no supports: 4 walls, 15% gyroid. Both tapers rise from
the bed at 45°, and the backs of the pockets and the stick slots bridge.

## Customizing

Cell count, cell diameter and length (up to about 69 mm for protected
button-top cells), the ribs' squeeze and the stick length. The 6" stick makes
a taller holder on three bands. The 3", 5" and 7" end in the middle of a band,
so the holder refuses them.

Not yet printed: the clearances and the crush ribs' squeeze are untested.
