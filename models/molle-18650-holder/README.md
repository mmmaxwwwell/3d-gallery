# MOLLE 18650 Holder

Four 18650 cells standing upright, each in a thin tube of its own, the tubes
fused side by side onto a MOLLE stick weave. The whole thing goes straight
onto PALS webbing with two of BigFootPL's
[Molle sticks](https://www.printables.com/model/411201-molle-stick) (in the
gallery's Library, `molle-stick`), the way a MOLLE pouch does. There is
nothing to screw together.

## Mounting

- Print two **3"** sticks. The holder covers one PALS band, two columns
  across (84.4 × 90 × 31 mm).
- Hold the holder against the webbing with its band window over a band: one
  block sits in the PALS gap above it, one in the gap below.
- Push each stick down through the slot in the top block, behind the band
  and into the bottom block, until its tip sits on the floor there. The hook
  springs over the top block's back wall.
- The stick's folded head drops into the cradle on top. Press it down until
  the domes on the cradle's cheeks click into the ends of the head's lanyard
  hole.
- To take it off, pull the head up out of the cradle and the stick out.

## Cells

Drop the cells into the tubes. Each sits on the cone at the bottom of its tube
and stands 8 mm proud of the rim, so you can pinch it out. Two small crush
ribs in each tube hold the cell. A drain hole at the tip of each cone lets
water out, and a pen pushed up through it frees a stuck cell.

## Printing

PETG, standing on its bottom, no supports: 4 walls, 15% gyroid. The tubes
and stick slots run straight up, the cones under the tubes grow at 45°, and
the top block's underside over the band is 45°. Every outside edge is
rounded.

## Customizing

Cell count, cell diameter and length (up to about 69 mm for protected
button-top cells), the ribs' squeeze, the wall, how far the cells stand
proud, and the stick length: 3" on one band, 4" or 5" on two, 6" or 7" on
three.

Not yet printed: the clearances, the crush ribs' squeeze and the cradle's
snap are untested.
