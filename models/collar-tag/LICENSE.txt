Notes
-----

The outline was authored in-repo (not sourced from any third-party SVG
library) because none of the public bean silhouettes we surveyed had a
clean single-color outline path: Openclipart's kidney beans are 3D-
shaded gradient renders, and SVG Repo's coffee-bean icons were rate-
limited on download. Rather than distort the source's intent, we drew
our own outline via bezier control points, checked it in as `bean.svg`
alongside `bean_bezpath` in the .scad lib, and CC0-dedicated it here so
downstream users have the same freedom to remix.
