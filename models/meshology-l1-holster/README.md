# Meshology L1 Holster

A holster for the Meshology L1 Meshtastic radio, printed in TPU 64D. The radio slides down into it from the top. The back, sides and floor are solid; the front is a big cutout so the controls stay visible and usable, with 8 mm of wall reaching in from each side (and 8 mm of sill above the floor) to hold the radio in. The edge of the front opening and the rim round the top are rounded over so they aren't sharp.

A MOLLE mount for the back is still to come.

## Printing

- TPU 64D, one piece, standing on its floor with the open top up. No supports: every wall is vertical.
- About 28 g of TPU.
- Four 5 mm drainage holes in the floor, edges chamfered.
- The wall is 2.4 mm all the way round, bottom corners included, except a 45° skirt where the rounded bottom meets the bed, so it prints without supports.

The top slopes from front to back. The rim drops at about 44° from the back to the front lips, which stop about 41 mm below the radio's top and keep sloping down toward the opening. That leaves the top of the radio out where you can grab it.

## Locking tab

The back wall comes up over the back of the radio, following the rounded corners of its back face, and three 10 mm tabs across the top bend over the radio's top back bevel to keep it in. The back's shoulders rise to guards either side of the tabs, level with their tops, so a strap or branch can't catch them. Slits between and beside them let each tab spring back: push the radio down and it clicks in; pull the tabs back to lift it out.

## Fit

The channel is the radio's shape plus 0.3 mm on every face (`tolerance`), including its rounded corners (R11 seen from the front) and the bevels round its front and back faces. The floor curves up into the corners and ramps up the bevels to meet them. The diagonal faces' size hasn't been measured yet (it's a 6 mm guess front-to-back per face), so check that first on a test print.
